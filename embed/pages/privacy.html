Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Messing, P.C. d/b/a Messing Law (“Messing Law,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through www.messing.law (the “Site”), how we use it, and the choices you have.

No attorney-client relationship

Contacting us through the Site, by email, or by scheduling a call does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please do not send confidential or sensitive information until we have confirmed in writing that we represent you.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form, sign up for updates, or schedule a consultation, we collect what you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, the topic of your inquiry, your message, and how you found us.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the third-party services embedded on the Site may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and the date and time of your visit.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and evaluate whether we can assist you, including checking for conflicts of interest
  • To schedule and hold consultations
  • To send news and updates you have asked to receive
  • To operate, secure, and improve the Site
  • To comply with our legal and professional obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

The Site uses third-party services that may process information about your visit under their own privacy policies, including:

  • Calendly, for scheduling consultations
  • Adobe Fonts and Google Fonts, to display the Site’s typefaces
  • Our website host and form-processing provider, to deliver the Site and the messages you send us

We may also share information with professional advisers, or where required by law, court order, or applicable rules of professional conduct.

Cookies

We do not use advertising cookies. Embedded services such as Calendly may set cookies needed for them to work. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function properly.

Data retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law and our professional obligations. We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from updates at any time using the link in any message or by contacting us.
  • You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information you have provided, subject to our legal and professional obligations. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy laws.

Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it was last revised.
